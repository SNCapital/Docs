    Research log — July 2026 carry round¶

    Method: strategy-research-methodology.md. Data: binance/ (17 spot large-caps, 30-min Go metrics, 2024-12-01..2026-05-29). Split: TRAIN 2024-12..2025-10, VALIDATION 2025-11..2026-05 (7 months, held out). Costs: fee 1.6 bps + half quoted spread + 1 bps slippage, next-bar VWAP fills.

    What was searched¶

    ~2,500 candidates: 41 base signals x spans (2-40d) x bands (0/0.3/0.6) x weighting (proportional / inverse-vol) x universe (all / 10 tightest-spread), plus ensembles, time-scale splits, seasonality, cluster relative value. Multiple-testing note: at this count the best pure-noise candidate on 11 months of daily PnL sits near Sharpe ~1.6-1.8; the train gate stayed at 2.0 and family grid-stability (median across the whole grid) was required on top, precisely because of this.

    What survived, what died¶

    • Liquidity/carry complex — survived. Amihud, depth, spread, vol, beta rank-carries and their blends: train Sharpe 1.9-3.1 across the whole parameter grid (not a lucky cell), both train halves positive, validation positive (family medians +0.5..+1.3). Everything that passed the gates belongs to this one complex (pairwise daily-PnL corr 0.4-0.98).
    • Book pressure — only inside the blend. Standalone book-imbalance signals peaked at train ~1.9 and validated at ~0; blended with carry they add on both windows.
    • Died OOS: distance-from-high (val -0.9), levels-below/above-5% (val -1.4), book-state time-splits, flow imbalances, seasonality — grid-unstable or sign-flipped on validation. Not shipped.
    • Momentum regime-flipped: weak on train (median 0.5), strong on validation (median 2.2-2.3). Fails the train gate, so not shipped — flagged as the first candidate for the next round precisely because it diversifies carry.

    Round 3 — the <=10-day lookback constraint¶

    After round 2 a hard product constraint landed: no strategy may look back more than 10 days (every rolling window / EMA span parameter <= 10d). The full search was re-run under the cap (2,460 candidates): same verdict — only the carry/book complex passes the gates with grid-stable families. The 90d distance-from-high died; its 10d analog (proximity to the 10-day high) turned out to be the best out-of-sample sleeve of all.

    All windows and spans <= 10 days.

    Portfolio (production run: strategies + Markowitz)¶

    python tools/backtest.py --metrics-roots binance/data-historical-vision binance/datametrics-1m --since 2024-12-01 --until 2026-05-29 --alphas carry_liquidity carry_core carry_quality carry_book_blend --execution vwap --max-w 0.125 --slippage-bps 1.0

    Full period (18 months, includes the held-out window), net of costs, at gross exposure 1.0: Sharpe 1.92, PnL +56% (~37%/yr), max DD -11.3%, turnover 0.29/day. Research split of the equal-weight blend: train Sharpe 2.76 (+37%/yr), validation Sharpe 0.87 (+13%/yr). The constrained (<=10d) set beats the unconstrained round-2 set out-of-sample — shorter memory adapts faster to the regime change in the validation window.

    Honest read & the 100%/yr target¶

    The train numbers meet the brief; the validation window does not. The carry complex is real (it survives every robustness cut), but its edge in Nov-2025..May-2026 was roughly a third of the train-period edge — regime dependence, not noise. Realistic forward expectation is Sharpe ~1-1.5 at ~15-25%/yr on gross 1.0.

    Leverage math at the full-period numbers: 100%/yr needs ~2.7x leverage -> levered max DD ~-30% and a Sharpe still ~1.9. That is survivable but brutal; at the validation-period edge it would be ~10x and ruinous. The honest lever for 100%/yr is not leverage on this portfolio — it is breadth: 17 names is tiny for a cross-sectional book (Sharpe scales ~sqrt(breadth) at fixed signal quality), and the parser already collects the top-100 universe going forward. Second lever: a momentum sleeve (validated-regime diversifier) once it can clear the gate on a longer train window.

    Validation-integrity disclosure¶

    The holdout was scored once per sweep round and family-level medians were inspected while deciding what families to ship (2 looks total before the final scoring). The shipped set was still ranked and parameterised on train only, but the validation Sharpe reported here is mildly optimistic as an unbiased estimate. Next data extension shifts the split forward and re-tests untouched months.

Five of them (smart_money_flow, oi_shock_rev, crowd_contra, oi_churn_regime, smart_dumb_acct) need USD-M futures panels (open interest, long/short ratios) that the local binance/ spot metrics do NOT contain — untestable and untradable here until that data lands. The four testable ones, on our split and costs:

    Swept over our <=10d grid none of them clears val>=1.5 (best: small_large_flow_div@3d_b100, train 2.35 / val 1.15). None shipped.

    New selection rule (user-set): train Sharpe >= 1.5 AND validation Sharpe >= 1.5. The previous carry_core / carry_liquidity / carry_book_blend@5d sleeves fail it and were dropped. Survivors + newly-admitted (the relaxed train bar finally lets momentum in):

    Holdout is now burned. With validation inside the selection gate these val numbers are selection criteria, not out-of-sample estimates. The next honest test is the next unseen months of data.

The rolling-30d Markowitz allocator chases recent sleeve returns; the pre-cleanup research reached the same conclusion (EW / risk parity beat Markowitz walk-forward). Next step: expose an equal-weight / risk-parity combine mode in the pipeline.

    Round 5 — protocol v2 (pre-registered 3-way split), the clean run¶

    Protocol fixed BEFORE the sweep (see methodology): TRAIN 2024-12..2025-08 (search), TEST 2025-09..2025-12 (admission gate only), VAL 2026-01..2026-05 (opened once, at the very end; never computed during the sweep). 38 simple one-formula bases x 5 spans x 2 bands x 2 weightings x 2 universes = 1,520 candidates; all windows <= 10d; liquidity mask is a fraction (60%) of the universe and the beta proxy is the cross-sectional mean, so every sleeve transfers unchanged to the ~100-name production universe.

    Band answer (measured on TRAIN): the no-trade band lifts the mean candidate Sharpe from -0.62 to +0.08 by cutting turnover ~3x. It is a cost filter, and it stays.

Markowitz allocation is NOT the route: on real sleeves it loses to EW/RP.

    Round 6 — cost-model audit, Markowitz fix, |corr| < 0.4¶

    Cost audit (user: "numbers too depressive"). The quoted-spread metric (ob__minor__spread_bps__mean) is broken on this dataset: BTCUSDT has a NEGATIVE mean (-94.7 bps; 17% of bars negative, median 0) and thin alts are ~2x inflated vs. what takers actually paid (WIF quoted 18.2 vs effective 6.1 bps median; NEAR 6.3 vs 2.6; APT 5.0 vs 1.9; OP 7.8 vs 3.8). The cost model now uses the EFFECTIVE spread from the tape: (buy_vwap - sell_vwap)/vwap, 7d rolling median, clipped [0.2, 50] bps (alpha_engine/backtest/pnl.py::effective_spread_bps, wired into the pipeline with quoted-metric fallback). Fee 1.6 bps + 1 bps slippage and the decide-at-t / fill-at-VWAP(t+1) delay are unchanged — deliberately conservative. No double-counting found. Impact: EW book test Sharpe 1.96 -> 2.80 on comparable sleeves.

    Markowitz fix (user: "reducing Sharpe looks wrong"). Two real defects in the combine layer: (a) mu/cov were estimated on raw 30-min bars — far too noisy for a tangency mean estimate, so the allocator chased the last day's hot sleeve; (b) the warm-up rows were NaN, silently flattening the whole portfolio for the first lookback days. Fixed: estimation now runs on daily-aggregated sleeve PnL and the warm-up trades equal weight. On train+test the markowitz combine went from portfolio-destroying to competitive (test-window Sharpe 3.61, the best of the three modes). It still loses out-of-sample to EW/RP — that is tangency fragility with few sleeves, not a bug.

    Portfolio re-selected under pairwise TRAIN |corr| < 0.4 (user rule), same pre-registered gates, corrected costs:

    name train test turnover
    carry_book_blend (carry+book, liq, 3d, b60) 3.53 1.13 0.44
    carry_core (5-way carry, 3d, b60) 2.54 2.01 0.19
    book_curvature (inv-vol, liq, 2d, b60) 2.30 1.47 0.61
    dist_high10 (10d-high proximity, inv-vol, liq, 5d, b60) 1.69 1.09 0.38

    Max pairwise |corr| = 0.40. EW: train 4.43, test 2.80.

The stable conclusions: costs are now honest and slightly LOWER than before (the depressive numbers were not a cost bug — the val regime is genuinely hard for the carry complex); EW/RP remain the production allocators; Markowitz is fixed but should not be the default. Next real measurement: the 100-name universe / new months.

    Rounds 7-8 — the walk-forward machine and the 14-sleeve book¶

    Goal (user): 20+ sleeves, pairwise |corr| <= 0.4, Sharpe >= 4 on data nothing was fitted to. Approach: a sleeve LIBRARY with canonical fixed parameters (~40 one-formula bases x {full / liquid-half / illiquid-half} + session-refresh and time-scale-split variants; no grids at all), and a monthly WALK-FORWARD: each month the portfolio is re-selected using only prior data (window Sharpe gate + greedy corr<=0.4 + risk-parity weights) and trades the next month. The concatenated 12 months are genuinely out-of-sample and hyperopt-free.

    Measured: - Walk-forward OOS, 12 months: Sharpe 1.41 (halves 0.71 / 2.23), ann +10.8%, maxDD -4.1%, ~14 sleeves/month. - Selection avg pairwise corr 0.06 -> diversification ceiling ~4x; the implied honest per-sleeve OOS Sharpe is ~0.4-0.6, capping any portfolio of these sleeves at OOS ~1.5-2.5 on 17 names regardless of N. - Negative results, disclosed: 6-name tercile universes and a nested-tuned selector on a 272-sleeve library made walk-forward WORSE (0.17-0.33) — selection noise dominates; bigger pools need bigger universes, not more variants. - Corr saturation: under |corr|<=0.4 this universe supports ~14 genuinely distinct sleeves, not 20+. The cap binds on mechanisms, not effort.

    Shipped: alpha_engine/strategies/book2026.py — spec-driven, 14 sleeves (book pressure x sessions, donchian/momentum by liquidity half, curvature, depth carry, OBV corr, slope, top-1 notional, whale prints, reversal-US, ts-split). Bit-for-bit parity with research (max daily-PnL diff 5e-7). Production run (--combine risk_parity): full-period net Sharpe 3.49, +38%, maxDD -3.0%, turnover 0.27/day — an IN-SAMPLE-SELECTED number (canonical params soften but do not remove selection bias); the honest forward expectation for this machine on 17 names is the walk-forward 1.41.

    Route to OOS >= 4 stays breadth: on ~100 names each sleeve gains ~sqrt-breadth (x2.0-2.4) AND the universe supports 5-6 disjoint liquidity groups (more sleeves, lower avg corr). The identical walk-forward machine re-run on the 100-name metrics is the next measurement.

    Round 9 — new mechanism families, the 20-sleeve book¶

    Response to "the formulas were lazy": 18 genuinely new mechanism families were designed and tested (canonical params, honest costs, 3 universes): BTC/liquid-half lead-lag, order-flow-vs-depth (OFI), book consumption and replenishment (d_size x flow sign), 8h funding-cycle echo, microprice drift, institutional footprint (q99/q50 trade sizes), impact asymmetry, book-mass location (weighted_distance), depth_ratio imbalance, flow persistence, tail-vs-mean pressure, absorption, flow-volatility carry, resilience trend, trade-mix shift.

    Survivors (stable in both halves): flow_vol_carry (1.78), mass_dist (1.59 / 1.21), ofi on illiquids (0.98), resil_trend (0.88). Dead, for the record: lead-lag at 30-min bars (sub-bar phenomenon), flow persistence, impact asymmetry, consumption/replenishment, funding echo (half-unstable).

    The new mechanisms unlocked the correlation saturation: the full-window book under pairwise |corr| <= 0.4 grew from 14 to 20 sleeves (avg corr 0.055, max 0.39). book2026.py updated; bit-for-bit parity for all 20. Full-period risk-parity: research 3.67 (ann +23.5%, maxDD -2.1%).

    Honest read stands: full-window sleeve Sharpes (0.5-2.0) are fixed-parameter but window-selected; the walk-forward machine remains the honest expectation (~0.7-1.4 on 17 names). Individual sleeve Sharpe 2.5+ with honest evaluation does not exist in this dataset — 60+ formula families tried across 9 rounds. Breadth remains the lever.

    Round 10 — the clean book (full universe only) + creative round 2¶

    User call: on 17 names sub-universe slicing is pseudo-diversification — dropped. 15 more new mechanisms tried (full universe, canonical params): anchored-VWAP gap, liquidity momentum (deepening book / compressing spread / improving amihud), churn carry, skew carry, BTC decoupling, regional (Asia-vs-US) footprint, crowd-vs-whale divergence, book-lean persistence, vol term structure, session variants, candle-body pressure, net-flow trend. Survivors: skew_carry (0.75, stable, corr 0.14 to the book) and lean_persist (1.17, stable — replaced book_ens_us which it dominates at corr 0.49). Dead: 13 of 15, including all "liquidity momentum" variants (-1.3..-1.5) and anchored-VWAP gap (-2.6).

    FINAL PRODUCTION BOOK (book2026.py): 6 full-universe strategies — mass_dist 1.59, depth_carry 1.35, mom_9d 1.28, lean_persist 1.17, notional_top1 0.83, skew_carry 0.75. Avg pairwise corr -0.02, max 0.28. Pipeline run (risk-parity): net Sharpe 3.52, +39.6% total, maxDD -3.8%, turnover 0.37/day. Same disclaimers as ever: full-window selection; walk-forward expectation ~1-1.5; next honest measurement = the 100-name universe, where sub-universes and the 20+ book become legitimate again.
