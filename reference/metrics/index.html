    Metrics catalogue¶

    Every key you can pass to data["{exchange}"]["{metric}"]. Generated from the *_METRICS registries in alpha_engine/metrics/*_aggregator.py.

    Klines (klines)¶

    OHLCV data — exists for both spot and USD-M.

    Metric Source
    close_price bar close
    open_price bar open
    high_price bar high
    low_price bar low
    volume base-asset volume
    volume_usd quote-asset volume (Binance's quote_volume)

    Up to NSC-9 the klines aggregator also exposed mark_price / index_price as aliases of the bar close. They have moved to the markprice kind below — at stress moments the last trade and the real mark price diverge, and the alias was producing false signals.

    Mark price (markprice)¶

    USD-M only. Sourced from the <symbol>@markPrice@1s WebSocket stream written to data/market=usdm/type=markprice/. The aggregator keeps the last-known value at every 30-min boundary.

    Metric Meaning
    mark_price Real mark price (basis-aware, used for liquidations)
    index_price Underlying spot index used to compute mark price
    predicted_funding Predicted rate for the next 8h settlement
    next_funding_time_ms Settlement timestamp the predicted rate refers to

    Funding (funding)¶

    USD-M only — realised settlement only. Predicted funding has moved to the markprice kind above.

    Metric Meaning
    realized_funding Last realised settlement rate

    Trades (trades)¶

    Per-bar metrics computed from raw trade-tape ticks. Quantile thresholds q ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9, 0.95} are evaluated per bar on the trade-size distribution. The threshold q is the per-bar percentile — buy_cost_quantile_0.95 sums the notional of buy trades whose own notional is at or above the 95th-percentile of the bar's buy-trade distribution (i.e. the top ~5% by size).

    Aggregates¶

    Metric Meaning
    volume Total base-asset volume in the bar
    buy_volume Volume from aggressor-buy trades
    sell_volume Volume from aggressor-sell trades
    buy_cost Notional ($) of aggressor-buy trades
    sell_cost Notional ($) of aggressor-sell trades
    buy_count Number of aggressor-buy trades
    sell_count Number of aggressor-sell trades
    vwap Volume-weighted average price
    buy_vwap VWAP across buy trades only
    sell_vwap VWAP across sell trades only
    open / close / high / low OHLC from trades (not the kline), averaged across buy/sell
    volatility Intra-bar std of minute log-returns (ddof=1, NaN if <3 non-empty minutes)
    hh Herfindahl index of trade-size concentration
    hh_buy Same, restricted to buys
    hh_sell Same, restricted to sells
    kyle_lambda Kyle's lambda (price impact regression slope on log-returns)
    max_buy_trade_cost Largest single buy trade ($)
    max_sell_trade_cost Largest single sell trade ($)
    asr_count Adverse-selection ratio by count (60s cross-bar lookahead)
    asr_volume Adverse-selection ratio by volume

    Count-above-threshold — T ∈ {100, 1000, 10000, 100000, 1000000}¶

    For each USD threshold T:

    Metric pattern Meaning
    buy_count_above_T Number of buy trades with notional strictly > T
    sell_count_above_T Same for sells

    Quantile metrics — q ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9, 0.95}¶

    For each q:

    Metric pattern Meaning
    buy_cost_quantile_{q} USD notional sum from buys at or above the q-th percentile by notional
    sell_cost_quantile_{q} Same for sells
    buy_trade_cost_quantile_{q} The q-th percentile boundary itself (single value, USD)
    sell_trade_cost_quantile_{q} Same for sells
    buy_volume_quantile_{q}_usd Alias of buy_cost_quantile_{q} (v1-bridge)
    sell_volume_quantile_{q}_usd Same
    buy_volume_quantile_{q} Base-asset volume version
    sell_volume_quantile_{q} Same
    buy_count_quantile_{q} Number of buys above the q-th percentile
    sell_count_quantile_{q} Same
    volume_quantile_{q} / _usd Overall volume version (no side)
    count_quantile_{q} Overall count version (no side)

    So data["binance-f"]["buy_cost_quantile_0.95"] is the per-bar USD spent by aggressor-buys whose own notional is at or above the 95th-percentile of the bar's buy distribution.

    Trades multilevel (trades_multilevel)¶

    Per-bar metrics computed on logical trades — chains of contiguous raw fills that share side, timestamp, and consecutive trade_ids with monotonic price. Suffix _ml = multilevel (chain length ≥ 2), _sl = single-level (chain length = 1).

    Quantile threshold q ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9, 0.95}. 73 keys total.

    Metric pattern Meaning
    {buy,sell}_count_{ml,sl} Chain counts per side
    {buy,sell}_cost_{ml,sl} Chain notional totals
    mean_cost_{ml,sl} Mean cost per chain (overall)
    {buy,sell}_vwap_{ml,sl} VWAP across chains, per side
    {buy,sell}_cost_quantile_{q}_{ml,sl} Top-q sums of chain notionals
    {buy,sell}_trade_cost_quantile_{q}_{ml,sl} q-th percentile boundary
    {buy,sell}_mean_price_delta_ml_quantile_{q} Mean (price_last − price_first) / price_first × side_sign for chains above q, ml only

    Identity invariant (pinned by tests): buy_cost == buy_cost_ml + buy_cost_sl.

    Orderbook (orderbook)¶

    Snapshot-based, multi-aggregated across the bar's snapshots. Depth percentages d ∈ {0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100} are percent-of-mid — bid_25_cost_avg is the bar-mean of bid liquidity within 25% of the running mid. Underscores in metric names come from str.replace(".", "_"), so 0.5 becomes 0_5.

    Aggregation suffix packs¶

    Each per-snapshot base metric is expanded into one of two packs:

    • 9-agg pack (_avg, _quantile_25, _quantile_50, _quantile_75, _quantile_90, _quantile_95, _skew, _min, _max): drops NaN samples only. Used for level / state metrics.
    • 10-agg pack (= 9-agg + _sum): additionally drops zero samples. Used for cross-snapshot diff / log-return metrics where 0 means "no observation".

    Best level (9-agg)¶

    Base metric Pack
    best_bid_price 9-agg
    best_ask_price 9-agg
    best_bid_size 9-agg
    best_ask_size 9-agg
    bid_ask_spread_abs 9-agg — absolute spread best_ask − best_bid
    bid_ask_spread_rel 9-agg — relative spread spread / mid
    last_best_bid_price last snapshot in the bar (scalar)
    last_best_ask_price last snapshot in the bar (scalar)

    Per-depth metrics (9-agg)¶

    For each depth d, side s ∈ {bid, ask}:

    Base metric Meaning
    {s}_{d}_cost USD notional within d% of mid on side s
    {s}_{d}_size Base-asset size within d% of mid
    {s}_{d}_vwap VWAP of liquidity within d%
    {s}_{d}_levels_size_count Number of populated levels within d%
    {s}_{d}_levels_size_count_nonzero Count of levels with size > 0
    {s}_{d}_levels_size_mean Mean size per level within d%
    cost_imbalance_{d}_ratio (bid_cost − ask_cost) / (bid_cost + ask_cost)
    cost_imbalance_{d}_norm Normalised variant: 1.0 if ask_cost = 0, NaN if both = 0
    vwap_{d}_midprice (bid_vwap + ask_vwap) / 2 per snapshot

    Cross-snapshot diff / log-return (10-agg)¶

    Base metric Meaning
    {s}_{d}_cost_diff Δ cost vs. previous snapshot
    {s}_{d}_size_diff Δ size vs. previous snapshot
    {s}_{d}_price_logreturn log-return of cumulative VWAP
    best_{s}_size_diff Δ best-level size
    best_{s}_price_logreturn log-return of best price

    Liquidity-shock — d ∈ {0.05, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 5, 25, 50} (9-agg)¶

    liq_shock_{side}_{d} = {side}_{d}_size[t] / rolling_mean(10s of {side}_{d}_size).

    Base metric Meaning
    liq_shock_bid_{d} Bid-side instantaneous liquidity vs 10s baseline
    liq_shock_ask_{d} Ask-side liq vs baseline
    liq_shock_ratio_{d} liq_shock_bid / (liq_shock_bid + liq_shock_ask)

    Liquidations (liquidations)¶

    Per-bar metrics on liquidation fills, prefixed liquidations_. Reader maps cum_filled_qty → size, avg_price → price (per fill, not the order intent), then filters size > 0. Same algorithmic template as trades — 50 keys total. Examples:

    Metric Meaning
    liquidations_{buy,sell}_count Count of long/short liquidations
    liquidations_{buy,sell}_count_above_{T} T ∈
    liquidations_{buy,sell}_cost Notional sums
    liquidations_{buy,sell}_cost_quantile_{q} q ∈
    liquidations_max_{buy,sell}_trade_cost Largest single liquidation ($)
    liquidations_vwap, _buy_vwap, _sell_vwap VWAPs
    liquidations_open/high/low/close OHLC, NaN if either side empty
    liquidations_volatility Intra-bar minute log-return std
    liquidations_hh, _hh_buy, _hh_sell HH on size (per L_T2.8)
    liquidations_kyle_lambda Kyle's lambda
    liquidations_asr_count, _asr_volume 60s cross-bar lookahead

    Maker orders (maker_orders)¶

    Spot-only (Binance spot). Each raw trade reconstructs to one maker_fill with the maker side inverted from taker: taker BUY → maker ASK, taker SELL → maker BID. ASR sign convention is inverted vs. taker (ask-maker "wins" when price falls).

    Prefix maker_ everywhere — deliberate deviation from generic bid_*/ask_* to avoid namespace collision with orderbook. 50 keys with the same structural template as trades/liquidations. Examples:

    Metric Meaning
    maker_{ask,bid}_count Maker fills counted on each side
    maker_{ask,bid}_count_above_{T} T ∈
    maker_{ask,bid}_cost_quantile_{q} q ∈
    maker_max_{ask,bid}_trade_cost Largest single maker fill ($)
    maker_vwap, maker_ask_vwap, maker_bid_vwap Maker-side VWAPs
    maker_volatility, maker_kyle_lambda Identical algos to taker template
    maker_asr_count, maker_asr_volume 60s lookahead, sign inverted

    Identity invariants (pinned): maker_ask_cost == buy_cost, maker_bid_cost == sell_cost, maker_vwap == vwap.

    Market stats (market_stats)¶

    USD-M only. Polled via REST (Binance futures + spot margin endpoints), not WebSocket. Futures coroutine runs at 5-min UTC-aligned cadence; spot interest at 1h cadence. Aggregator does resample("30min").last() .ffill().reindex(idx).ffill().

    Metric Source endpoint
    open_interest /fapi/v1/openInterest
    global_long_accounts /futures/data/globalLongShortAccountRatio
    top_long_accounts /futures/data/topLongShortAccountRatio
    top_long_positions /futures/data/topLongShortPositionRatio
    daily_interest_rate /sapi/v1/margin/next-hourly-interest-rate × 24 (linear, no compounding). Gated MARGIN_INTEREST_ENABLED, opt-in.

    How resolution works¶

    The lazy dict (AggregatedMetricsDict) maps a metric name to an aggregator group via prefix rules in metrics_dict._resolve_source:

    Prefix / pattern Group
    liquidations_* liquidations
    maker_* maker_orders
    {open_interest, global_long_*, top_long_*, daily_interest_rate} market_stats
    *_ml / *_sl / mean_cost_{ml,sl} / *_mean_price_delta_ml_* trades_multilevel
    bid_* or ask_* containing cost / size / vwap / levels / spread / logreturn orderbook
    cost_imbalance_*, vwap_*_midprice, liq_shock_*, last_best_* orderbook
    buy_* or sell_* trades
    close_price, volume_usd, … klines
    mark_price, index_price, next_funding_time_ms, predicted_funding markprice
    anything else containing funding funding

    Names that don't match raise KeyError — the dict refuses to guess. To add a new metric whose name doesn't fit, also add a branch in _resolve_source.
