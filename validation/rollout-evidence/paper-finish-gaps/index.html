    Paper-finish source gaps and restored evidence¶

    This inventory supplements the root-owned remaining-work register; it does not change that register's status or close any source-coverage item. It uses saved committed reports and named, bounded evidence files only. No raw archive or B2 listing/read was performed for this report. A recorded copy, ACK floor, upload log, Parquet checksum, or count is not treated as source-to-archive event reconciliation.

    The machine ledger is /opt/trading/codex-operations/f3-inventory/paper-finish-gaps.json. unknown means saved evidence does not establish either recovery or loss. A checksum of an evidence report is distinguished from a checksum of the underlying source data.

    Imported history quality. The PostgreSQL history contains 488,604 rows for 120 symbols, from 2026-06-16 22:00 UTC through 2026-09-28 14:30 UTC. Market per row, per-row source identity and a dataset checksum were not captured in the evidence used here. The quality state is unknown/full-blind, not GOOD; possible effects are stale or incomplete warmup, universe and signal inputs. No reimport or retrospective quality certification was performed.

    • The bounded 2026-09-30 04:00–05:00 staging pilot completed: 371 finalized files, 363,115,743 bytes and 2,797,302 rows in the event-time interval (1,470 selected-segment rows were outside that hour). The file-set SHA-256 is 0ecfed83d7cb88a543bfaf49596f4f64dac20630d47f246a3007acc4d36a368f as recorded by the tool manifest at /opt/trading/codex-operations/f3-inventory/storage-pilot-r2-20260930-0400.json (manifest SHA-256 6baefd33f28f61c8187de2f7e280ba066287c8a0684e2b2deda99aa0b71c995a).     • The 2026-09-29 snapshot audit's tiny fixtures passed native/API comparisons for their constructed cases, including concurrent suffix behavior. They establish verifier behavior for those fixtures only, not original-snapshot membership or prefix recovery.
    • Saved operations reported nine 2026-09-29 merged-kind objects as uploaded+verified; the current evidence still lacks full remote GetObject row-identity and payload-digest reconciliation. No B2 API read was made for this inventory.

    The pilot verifies a closed local staging interval, not a repaired source gap. None of these positive checks turns an unknown interval into complete or GOOD history.
